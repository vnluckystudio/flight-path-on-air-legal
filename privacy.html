Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 · Developer: Lucky Studio

Lucky Studio respects your privacy. This policy describes how Flight Path On Air handles information when you use the app.

Location and flight records

Flight Path On Air requests location permission to show your current GPS position and to record a flight when you choose to start recording. An active recording can continue in the background if you lock the screen or switch apps. High-accuracy background tracking stops when you pause or end a flight.

GPS points, flight sessions, and related statistics are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to Lucky Studio. The app does not require an account. Removing the app may remove flight data stored on the device.

Airport catalog and maps

When the app returns to the foreground, it may contact OurAirports to check for an updated public airport catalog. This request does not include your location or flight records. The route map uses bundled map data and works offline; it does not request map tiles.

Purchases

Flight Path On Air uses Apple StoreKit to check and restore the one-time Record Unlock purchase. Apple processes App Store transactions under its own privacy terms. Lucky Studio does not receive your payment card details.

Advertising and analytics

Flight Path On Air does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or an account service.

Your choices

You can manage location access in iOS Settings. You can pause or end recording in the app at any time. GPX and CSV exports are created when you request them and shared through the iOS share interface you select.
